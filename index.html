    How it works

    • Every rod is a place value. The right-most rod is ones, then tens, then hundreds.
    • Each bottom bead counts 1. The top bead counts 5.
    • A bead only counts when it touches the middle bar.
    • To make 7, slide the top bead down and two bottom beads up.
    • Bead colours follow the Indian number groups: hundreds, thousands, lakhs and crores.
